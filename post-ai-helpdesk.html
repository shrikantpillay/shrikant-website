What I Learned Building an AI Helpdesk with Copilot Studio, Intune and ServiceNow

October 2026 · AI · Digital Workplace

Recently, I explored how an AI-based virtual assistant could move beyond simply answering IT support questions and actually participate in resolving endpoint issues.

The objective was straightforward: a user reports a problem such as "My VPN is not working", and the assistant should understand the issue, identify the likely cause, recommend a fix, and where appropriate trigger remediation through endpoint management tools.

The Architecture

The solution can combine Copilot Studio as the conversational layer, Microsoft Intune for endpoint actions, a knowledge repository for troubleshooting guidance, and ServiceNow for ITSM integration.

What I Learned

One of the important lessons was that the AI assistant should not try to solve every issue autonomously. The real value comes from orchestration: understanding the user context, selecting an approved remediation action, executing it safely, validating the outcome, and escalating when required.

Why This Matters

Traditional virtual assistants are largely focused on knowledge retrieval. The next stage is moving from answering questions to executing controlled actions across enterprise platforms.

This changes the conversation from "How many tickets can a chatbot answer?" to "How many support journeys can be resolved end-to-end without human intervention?"
