Recently, I explored how an AI-based virtual assistant could move beyond simply answering IT support questions and actually participate in resolving endpoint issues.

## The Architecture

The solution can combine Copilot Studio as the conversational layer, Microsoft Intune for endpoint actions, a knowledge repository for troubleshooting guidance, and ServiceNow for ITSM integration.

## What I Learned

The AI assistant should not try to solve every issue autonomously. The real value comes from orchestration: understanding context, selecting an approved remediation action, executing it safely, validating the outcome, and escalating when required.

## Why This Matters

The next stage of enterprise virtual assistants is moving from answering questions to executing controlled actions across enterprise platforms.
